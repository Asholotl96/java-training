Recursion:
> 6 Types
  - Direct Recursion: Calling the method within itself directly w new parameters.
  - Indirect Recursion: When 2 or more methods call each other in a loop until base case is achieved.
  - Tail Recursion: When the recursion is called at the end of the method (return statement).
  - Non-Tail Recursion: Anywhere but the tail.
  - Linear Recursion: When a method calls itself only once.
  - Binary/Tree Recursion: When a method calls itself more than once. 

> Catalan number series
  C0=1, C1=1
  C2=C0*C1 + C1*C2
  C3=C0*C2 + C1*C1 + C2*C0
  and so on
  General formula, Cn= (2n!)/((n+1)!n!)
